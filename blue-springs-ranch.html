About the community

A new community, offered the Westshore way

Blue Springs Ranch is Westshore Land Sales' newest Florida community — homesites and acreage tracts in a natural, peaceful setting. We're finalizing property details, pricing, and photos now, with more information coming soon.

Every purchase is handled with the same transparency and care that defines Westshore Land Sales — clear information, friendly guidance, and no pressure from your first question through closing.

Blue Springs Ranch is about 1 hour from Florida's Emerald Coast beaches, less than 10 minutes from the city of Marianna, and just 1 mile from the Blue Springs Recreational Area.

  • Ten acre and larger tracts
  • Professional property surveys
  • Title work and warranty deeds
  • Financing assistance available
  • Local, state, and federal approvals when required
