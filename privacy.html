Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Westshore Land Sales ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through westshorelandsales.com (the "Site"), how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

When you submit one of our contact or inquiry forms, we collect the information you provide, which may include your name, email address, phone number, and details about the property you're interested in. We also collect standard usage data automatically through analytics and advertising tools (see "Cookies and Analytics" below).

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiry and follow up about available land and communities
  • To send you marketing communications by email, phone call, or text message, where you've consented to receive them
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Site
  • To comply with legal obligations

SMS & Text Messaging

If you opt in to receive text messages from Westshore Land Sales, message frequency may vary and message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP, or reply HELP for assistance. No mobile opt-in information or consent is shared with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Cookies & Analytics

We use Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, and the Meta Pixel to understand how visitors use the Site and to measure the effectiveness of our advertising. These tools may use cookies or similar technology. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes. We may share information with service providers who help us operate our business (such as our customer relationship management platform) solely to perform services on our behalf, or when required by law.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information we collect. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

You may opt out of marketing emails using the unsubscribe link in any email, opt out of texts by replying STOP, or contact us directly to ask what information we have about you or to request that it be deleted.

Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
